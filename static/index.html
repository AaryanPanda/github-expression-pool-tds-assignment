How it works

Each photo has 78 Py-Feat numbers. The model also uses each number's rank among that person's 10 photos, giving 156 inputs. A logistic regression learns 10 × 156 weights. The grader scores every photo for every expression and assigns each expression to exactly one photo.

Accuracy is measured on people held out from training (5-fold, each person's 10 photos kept together). Marks scale (provisional): 0 at 60%, 5 at 70%, 10 at 80%+.
